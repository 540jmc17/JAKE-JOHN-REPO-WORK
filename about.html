CampusGear / About

Shared campus equipment, simplified.

CampusGear is Team 18's equipment borrowing project, designed to bring the student browsing experience and staff equipment workflows together.

What we're building

Students will be able to browse available equipment and, in later sprints, manage reservations and borrowing. Staff will be able to register and track equipment through role-appropriate tools.

This Sprint 1 website skeleton provides a shared layout and navigation. Database storage, login, privacy controls, equipment registration, and the live catalog are separate team assignments.
